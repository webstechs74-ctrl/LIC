Understand your options

Major LIC plan categories

Different life stages need different solutions. We help you understand the purpose of each category before you consider a policy.

Protection

Term Assurance

Life-cover focused options intended to support dependants financially in the event of the insured person's death, subject to policy terms.

Protection + Saving

Endowment Plans

Plans that combine life cover with disciplined long-term savings and policy-specific maturity benefits.

Periodic Goals

Money Back Plans

Policy options designed to provide survival benefits at specified intervals, along with applicable life cover.

Retirement

Pension & Annuity

Solutions intended for retirement preparation and policy-specific regular income or annuity options.

Important: Product availability, eligibility, premium, benefits and returns vary by plan. Please read the official benefit illustration, sales brochure and policy document before purchasing.
